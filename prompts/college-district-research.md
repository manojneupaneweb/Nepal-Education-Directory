## 4. REQUIRED PROFILE TOPICS

These ten topics are mandatory. Research each one on the web until that topic is either filled from sources or explicitly marked missing. Do not leave a topic blank because the homepage had no menu item for it. Check the college site, subpages, PDFs, Facebook, YouTube, Google Maps, news, and university pages.

### Overview

Who the college is, where it sits, who runs it, which university it belongs to, when it opened, and what it is known for. Nepali name, short description, detailed description, highlights, vision, mission, and leadership only when a public page states them.

Search: "[COLLEGE] [DISTRICT]", "[COLLEGE] about", "[COLLEGE] campus", "[COLLEGE] introduction", plus the Nepali name.

### Courses

Every higher-education program, not a sample. Degree name, level, faculty, duration, eligibility, entrance rule, published fee, and intake. Separate bachelor, master, diploma, and other programs. One prospectus page is not the end if the site or university lists more courses.

Search: "[COLLEGE] courses", "[COLLEGE] programs", "[COLLEGE] bachelor", "[COLLEGE] admission", "[COLLEGE] prospectus", "[COLLEGE] fee".

### Facilities

Classrooms, labs, library, hostel, transport, sports, cafeteria, internet, health room, and any other campus facility named in a public source. Do not invent facilities from a generic college description.

Search: "[COLLEGE] facilities", "[COLLEGE] hostel", "[COLLEGE] library", "[COLLEGE] lab", "[COLLEGE] infrastructure".

### Gallery

Direct image URLs of the campus, buildings, labs, events, and logo. Record the page each image came from. Skip stock photos and unrelated images. Keep searching albums and Facebook photo tabs when the website gallery is thin.

Search: "[COLLEGE] gallery", "[COLLEGE] photos", "[COLLEGE] campus photos", site path /gallery, Facebook photos.

### Videos

Official or clearly institutional video URLs: YouTube, Facebook video, TikTok, campus tour, admission video, event video. Title, URL, platform, and what the video shows. Do not stop after the first channel trailer if more public videos exist.

Search: "[COLLEGE] youtube", "[COLLEGE] campus tour", "[COLLEGE] video", site path /videos.

### Updates

Notices, news, results, admission announcements, events, and downloads. Date, title, summary, and source URL. Mark whether the item looks current or historical. Old notices stay in the record as historical. They are not described as open admissions.

Search: "[COLLEGE] notice", "[COLLEGE] news", "[COLLEGE] result", "[COLLEGE] admission notice", site paths /notice, /news, /updates.

### FAQs

Questions and answers published by the college, university, or a page that quotes the college. Keep the public meaning. If no FAQ page exists, set the array empty and say so in missing_information. Do not write FAQs from guesses.

Search: "[COLLEGE] FAQ", "[COLLEGE] frequently asked questions", site path /faq.

### Contact

Phone, mobile, email, admission contact, website, office hours, full address, ward, and map link. Check the contact page, footer, Google Maps, and the latest admission notice. Multiple numbers are kept. Unverified numbers are labeled unverified.

Search: "[COLLEGE] contact", "[COLLEGE] phone", "[COLLEGE] email", "[COLLEGE] address", Google Maps "[COLLEGE] [DISTRICT]".

### Reviews

Public reviews from Google Maps, Facebook recommendations, and other pages that name this exact college. Reviewer name if shown, rating if shown, date if shown, short text, platform, and URL. Do not invent reviews, average scores, or star ratings. A missing rating stays null.

Search: "[COLLEGE] reviews", "[COLLEGE] Google reviews", "[COLLEGE] rating", Google Maps place page.

### Q&A

Public questions and answers about this college: site Q&A, Facebook comments that the page itself answered, and published interview answers. Each item needs the question, the answer, who answered if known, and the source URL. Unanswered questions can be stored with a null answer. Do not answer the question yourself.

Search: "[COLLEGE] questions", "[COLLEGE] Q&A", "[COLLEGE] interview", Facebook Q&A or comments on the official page.

- Cross-check institutional identity, location, and affiliation.
- Record sources for each institution and for each of the ten topics.
- Do not stop when you find only 10 or 20 institutions.
